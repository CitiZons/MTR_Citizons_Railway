# Citizons Railway 0.1.5

[English](README.en.md) | [简体中文](README.zh-CN.md)

For Minecraft 1.20.1 / Forge 47.4.18 / [MTR](https://github.com/Minecraft-Transit-Railway/Minecraft-Transit-Railway) 4.0.3, paired with [MTR Railway Point Advanced](https://github.com/CitiZons/MTR_Railway_Point_Advanced) **0.1.7+**. Gauge: 1.435 m; rail-top height: 0.26428 m; rail-base height: 0.099422 m; repeat length: 0.6 m.

## Installation

Copy this folder or the versioned ZIP to the instance's `resourcepacks` folder and enable it. Install Point Advanced **0.1.7 or later**, with the same build on the client and server, and remove older JARs. If a turnout is locked to another style, select automatic or the matching Citizons style in the blueprint.

## Track styles

| MTR style | Structure |
|---|---|
| Citizons high-fidelity rail, ballast, 1435 mm (new / old) | Recessed central concrete sleepers, clip fasteners and arched trapezoidal ballast bed. |
| Citizons high-fidelity rail (outer guard), ballast, 1435 mm (new / old) | Two inner guard rails with shared reinforced seats; the ends taper inward toward the centre. |
| Citizons high-fidelity rail (central guard), ballast, 1435 mm (new / old) | Flat-top sleepers, a central guard rail, independent bolt plates and protected ends. |
| Citizons high-fidelity rail, slab with sleepers, 1435 mm | Continuous concrete slab and separate shoulder support blocks. |
| Citizons high-fidelity rail, direct support without sleepers, 1435 mm | Two continuous longitudinal concrete supports without a horizontal central slab. |

Each ballasted family has a `（新）` and an `(旧)` MTR entry, for 8 styles in total; both share geometry and differ only in atlas materials. Turnouts, slide plates, frogs, guard-rail seats and end transitions are generated by Point Advanced. The three bed types share the same fastener layout. At the V nose, only inner fasteners are omitted; outer guard-side fasteners remain, and matching gaps up to 2 cm in rail, guard-rail, ballast and concrete-bed joints are filled. With only this resource pack installed, MTR displays its static resource models and does not provide these procedural features.

`assets/mtrsteamloco/rails/citizons_railway.json` supplies the eight MTR style entries, all with an explicit `textureId`. Descriptors in `assets/citizons_railway/rail_profiles` identify rails, sleepers, fittings, beds, LODs and continuous guards. Every OBJ under `models/rail/new` and `models/rail/old` declares its own `mtllib` and resolves `map_Kd` to the matching variant atlas.

## Detail levels

Point Advanced switches between high, medium and low detail at **4 m / 12 m** by default; these values can be changed in the mod configuration. Face counts per 0.6 m repeating unit are:

| Style | High | Medium | Low |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Ballasted mainline | 1528 | 376 | 240 |
| Ballasted outer guard | 1232 | 446 | 298 |
| Ballasted central guard | 1810 | 530 | 298 |
| Slab with sleepers | 1210 | 285 | 213 |
| Direct support without sleepers | 1140 | 238 | 172 |

When the resource pack is used alone, MTR uses the high-detail model selected by the style entry.

All three ballasted sleeper LODs retain their full 120 mm body and textured sides, with the same rail-seat height and central depression (or flat top for central-guard sleepers). Lower LODs use fewer top-profile segments; medium and low detail use dedicated simplified fittings, and slab and direct-support sections are simplified. OBJ files share position, UV and normal indices. Point Advanced caches ordinary rails and supports in complete 8 m chunks.

## Regeneration and packaging

`VERSION` is the resource-pack version source; `pack_format: 15` is the Minecraft resource format. The generator and packaging scripts are in the project root. The texture atlas contains rail sides, polished heads, ends, concrete, fasteners and ballast. OBJ files use standard V coordinates with `flipV: true`. See [MODEL_NOTES.md](MODEL_NOTES.md) for dimensions and groups.

This project is primarily implemented with ChatGPT.
